Canasta Premier

Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 1, 2025  ·  Last updated: June 2026

The short version: Canasta Premier does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The app works completely offline and requires no account, login, or internet connection.

1. Who we are

Canasta Premier is an independent mobile card game application. References to "we," "us," or "our" in this policy refer to the developer of Canasta Premier.

2. Information we collect

We collect no personal information of any kind. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any contact information
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not collect payment information (purchases are handled entirely by Apple or Google)
  • We do not use analytics tools, tracking SDKs, or advertising SDKs
  • We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies
  • We do not collect device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • We do not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos

3. How the app stores data

Canasta Premier stores only your game preferences and progress (such as difficulty setting and current scores) locally on your device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is never transmitted to us or any third party
  • Can be deleted at any time by uninstalling the app

4. Third-party services

Canasta Premier does not integrate with any third-party services, advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social media platforms.

The only third parties involved are:

  • Apple App Store — handles payment processing for iOS purchases. Apple's privacy policy applies to that transaction and is available at apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Play Store — handles payment processing for Android purchases. Google's privacy policy applies to that transaction and is available at policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children's privacy

Canasta Premier is rated 4+ (Everyone) and is appropriate for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from any users, we do not collect any information from children either. We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and equivalent international regulations.

6. Data sharing and sale

We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any user data with any third party — because we collect none. There is nothing to share.

7. Data security

Because all data remains locally on your device and we collect nothing, there is no central database of user information that could be compromised. Your game data is as secure as your device itself.

8. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data under laws such as GDPR (European Union), CCPA (California), and similar regulations. Because we collect no personal data, these rights are automatically satisfied — there is no data to access, correct, export, or delete beyond what is stored locally on your own device.

9. Changes to this policy

If we ever update this privacy policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Any significant changes that affect data collection will be communicated through an app update notice. We will never introduce data collection without clearly notifying users first.
